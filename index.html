ProCardio / Final-year internship at Ciprotec

From procedure notes to review-ready reports.

Interventional cardiology teams record information throughout a procedure. When those details sit in different tools or formats, teams have to pull them together again for reporting.

What I built

I translated workflow requirements into a hospital-scoped web platform. I prepared imaging data, evaluated and integrated computer vision suggestions, and worked on document retrieval and report generation. Clinicians can accept, edit or reject suggested entries.

Potential impact

Reusing structured entries could reduce repeated documentation and make reports easier to review. Shared defaults and hospital-specific settings let the same application adapt to local workflows.

My Health Friend / Machine learning and data

Test personalization before rollout.

A wellness suggestion is easier to act on when it fits someone’s schedule and preferences.

What I built
Our team built a Flask planner and an activity-prediction pipeline with participant-separated evaluation. A separate contextual-bandit simulation tests how recommendations adapt to feedback.
Evaluation
The Random Forest activity classifier reached 0.731 ROC-AUC on a test split with six held-out participants.
Potential impact
Participant-level evaluation and simulation give teams a way to assess personalization ideas before testing them with users.
  • Flask
  • Pandas
  • scikit-learn
  • Random Forest
  • SQLite
